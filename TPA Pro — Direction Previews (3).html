TPA Pro — 3 design directions

Same dashboard content (KPIs + claims table + sidebar) rendered in three distinct schools. Pick a direction (or blend) and I'll build the full-app prototype rooted in it. Recommended: A for authority, B for all-day comfort, C for data-density.

Direction A

Swiss Editorial

Pentagram / Vignelli lineage. Black on cream, hairline rules, numbered sections. Keywords: structural · monochrome · grid-disciplined · quiet.
